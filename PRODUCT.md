# MERCADIA — Product

> *Rival merchant dynasties reshape a sunlit canal-and-tramway city era by era.*

MERCADIA is a deliberate, four-era isometric dynasty city tycoon for one to four players. Each
player leads a merchant House, claims blocks in a sunlit canal district, opens shops, adopts
lasting Dynasty Charters, and invests in civic placemaking. Citizens walk the streets with real
budgets and desires, choosing between rival doors every few seconds. Rather than frantic RTS
click-spam, the city breathes in a strategic Dynasty Civ cadence where long-horizon block
planning, era unlocks, and territorial patronage compound toward the final Crown Index reckoning.

## Who it is for

- A solo strategist who wants a deliberate ten-minute dynasty match against three AI Houses with
  distinct temperaments.
- Two to four friends sharing an invite link who want a tense, readable battle for canal
  districts, transit corridors, and Crown supremacy in the same live city.
- Anyone who enjoys watching a miniature city come alive: the title screen opens directly onto a
  running canal district.

## The promise

1. **Dynasty Civ cadence, not frantic clicking.** The match unfolds across four escalating Eras
   with a calm, legible street rhythm, deliberate AI pacing, and brief **Era Council** interludes
   where Houses adopt compounding Charters and unlock new civic and commercial tiers.
2. **Clarity in the first sixty seconds.** On opening a match, the objective, controls, and best
   first move are unmistakable without a wall of tutorial prose. A compact, self-retiring
   Founding Guide points to high-value starter corners, surfaces your first shop's live diagnosis,
   and disappears cleanly once you are underway.
3. **Unmistakable House ownership.** Every House flies a distinct heraldic crest alongside its
   color on tall building banners, bold lot kerbs, contiguous block borders, leaderboard rows, and
   event dispatches—so territorial control reads at a glance even when zoomed out.
4. **Threshold unlocks that evolve the district.** Advancing through Eras and crossing Dynasty
   Milestones visibly unlocks higher-tier shops, grand Level 3 architecture, civic landmarks, and
   livelier city traffic.
5. **A loud, legible Chronicle.** Major dynasty moves, Era transitions, Milestone unlocks, lead
   changes, Promo Blitzes, price-war undercuts, and city events fire punchy, impossible-to-miss
   dispatches with exact mechanical impact tags and click-to-focus jumps.
6. **The city is the interface.** The sunlit canvas fills the viewport, panels stay compact at the
   edges, and every ledger figure has a visible cause on the street.
7. **One click to play.** *Play Solo Now* launches a Standard four-era match immediately.
   *Create Room* yields a four-letter code and shareable invite link.

## Core loop

1. **Orient and claim (First 60 seconds).** A single-line objective confirms the win condition
   (*Highest Crown Index after Era IV*). The compact Founding Guide highlights prime starter lots
   with a warm brass pulse and invites your first shop. Build mode shows each vacant lot's
   bonuses (Corner, Canal-front, Transit) and live foot-traffic pips, while hovering a blueprint
   lists synergies, nearby competition, and civic reach.
2. **Build and carve territory.** Six shop archetypes serve six citizen desires; three civic
   projects reshape footfall. Adjacent lots claimed by the same House visually knit together into
   a contiguous House block, signalling territorial dominance and compounding synergy.
3. **Manage with live diagnosis.** Clicking an owned shop opens the Inspector with a crisp
   one-line diagnosis (`Passed by: 7 too pricey · 4 queue full`, `Off-peak — Coffee peaks in
   Morning`, `Undercut — 1 cheaper rival Bistro nearby`), price stance controls, upgrades, Promo
   Blitzes, restock, and sale.
4. **Advance through Eras and Milestones.** As each Era closes, the **Era Council** briefly opens
   to celebrate the new Era's unlocked buildings, summarize the Crown race, and let each House
   choose one permanent **Dynasty Charter** that shapes the rest of its strategy. Crossing
   cumulative Dynasty Milestones awards permanent House Crest stars, dividends, and banner
   flourishes.
5. **Read the Mercadia Chronicle.** City events forecast ahead of arrival with map markers on the
   affected landmark and shops; rivalries, Promo Blitzes, Milestone unlocks, and lead changes hit
   the Chronicle with crisp impact pills and click-to-focus navigation.
6. **Reckon the Dynasty.** When Era IV closes, **The Mercadia Gazette — Final Edition** crowns
   the winning House with its heraldic crest, match headline, exact Crown equation breakdown,
   era-by-era market share chart, and match honours.

## Systems

### Four Eras of the Dynasty

| Era | Name | Unlocks & Escalation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Era I** | Founding Charter | `Roastery`, `Bistro`, `Pocket Park`, Level 1–2 upgrades. Calm opening cadence to establish cash flow and claim foundational corners. |
| **Era II** | Transit & Trade | `Arcade`, `Nightmarket`, `Tram Stop` unlock. Commuter and evening waves swell; transit corridors become contested arteries. |
| **Era III** | Grand Boulevard | `Boutique`, `Cinema`, `Festival Plaza`, and Level 3 grand architecture unlock. Tourists and foodies arrive in force; prestige races intensify. |
| **Era IV** | Golden Dynasty | Full district prosperity, peak civic stakes, high-impact Gazette events, and the decisive final Crown Index reckoning. |

Locked build tools remain visible in the dock with an Era lock badge (`Era II`, `Era III`) and
tooltip so players plan treasury and real estate ahead of each transition. As the district
advances from Era I to Era IV, the city visibly matures with livelier crowds, extra harbor and
tram activity, and festive architectural trim.

### Dynasty Charters (Era Council)

At the close of Eras I, II, and III, the **Era Council** pauses the simulation for a deliberate
strategic beat. Each House selects one permanent **Dynasty Charter** from three distinct
doctrines—such as *Guild Artisanry* (stronger shop quality and upgrade efficiency), *Transit
Patronage* (cheaper civic builds and bonus civic prestige), *Merchant Credit* (lower loan
interest and leaner payroll), or *Canal Showcase* (stronger canal/plaza appeal and tourist draw).
In solo play the player advances when ready; in multiplayer a short countdown commits choices
automatically if a player waits, keeping the room flowing smoothly.

### Dynasty Milestones

Four cumulative thresholds reward compounding play across the match:

- **Charter Merchant** — Serve your first 25 citizens (`+§80` dividend, first Crest star).
- **Block Baron** — Hold 3 active properties or an adjacent 2-lot House enclave (`+4 Prestige`,
  territorial banner flourish).
- **Civic Benefactor** — Construct 2 civic projects or reach 18 Civic Prestige (`+§120` dividend,
  `+4 Prestige`).
- **District Sovereign** — Reach §1,200 Net Worth or 40% Market Share during Era III–IV
  (`+6 Prestige`, gilded Crest star).

Every Milestone triggers a loud Chronicle dispatch, adds a star to the House's crest on the
leaderboard, and enhances its banners on the map.

### The district

A 12×12 isometric canal district ringed by Streetcar Avenue along the harbor and bisected by the
Grand Canal with two avenue bridges and two arched stone footbridges. Forty-nine buildable lots
front camera-facing streets so queues remain legible. Townhouses emit residents; Grand Station
and the Harbor Pier emit tourists; brass-and-cream trams circle the avenue and stop at Tram
Stops.

### Citizens

| Archetype | Budget | Temperament |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commuter | §14–36 | Price-sensitive, impatient, coffee first |
| Local | §30–70 | Balanced; strolls to parks |
| Foodie | §50–120 | Pays for quality; dining and coffee |
| Tourist | §80–200 | Barely price-sensitive; fashion, dining, plazas |
| Night Owl | §40–110 | Evening nightlife, culture, leisure |

Each citizen carries a desire (`coffee`, `dining`, `leisure`, `fashion`, `culture`, `nightlife`),
budget, patience, and mood. Clicking a citizen opens a speech card showing their live state and
destination shop.

### Shops

| Shop | Desire | Era | Cost | Character |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Roastery | coffee | Era I | §180 | Fast morning rush, steady cash foundation |
| Bistro | dining | Era I | §280 | Lunch and dinner surges; thrives on canal and plaza frontage |
| Arcade | leisure | Era II | §260 | Afternoon and evening play; pairs with Tram Stops |

Every shop progresses through three architectural levels (Level 3 unlocks in Era III), four price
stances (Budget −25%, Fair, Craft +20%, Prestige +45%), stock management, wages, and rent.

### Civic projects

| Project | Era | Cost | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pocket Park | Era I | §160 | +20% quality pull for neighbouring shops; locals stroll here; prestige |
| Tram Stop | Era II | §240 | Trams pause and drop commuters on the block; transit bonus nearby; prestige |
| Festival Plaza | Era III | §340 | +30% appeal aura over two tiles; tourists gather here; highest prestige |

Each civic project's Inspector reports its concrete contribution: **Riders delivered** for a Tram
Stop, **Nearby shops boosted** and **Boosted sales** for a Park or Plaza.

### Money and treasury

Revenue arrives per sale; supplies are purchased via Manual Restock or Auto-Restock (+15%
convenience premium). Payroll deducts wages, rent, and loan interest on a steady, readable
cadence. The Emergency Credit Line lends §400 with its exact per-era interest rate stated on the
button. Selling returns 60% of invested capital. The Ledger drawer reconciles opening cash,
operations, Milestone dividends, financing, and closing cash to the exact §.

### City events and the Mercadia Chronicle

Sudden Cloudburst, Harbor Cruise Arrival, Free-Ride Tram Day, Food Critic Visit, Street Lantern
Festival, Commercial Rent Hike, and Supply Crunch forecast ahead of arrival with map callouts and
print a closing receipt when finished. Alongside city events, the **Mercadia Chronicle** surfaces
Era transitions, Milestone unlocks, Charter adoptions, Crown lead changes, Promo Blitzes, and
nearby price-war undercuts in a punchy, high-contrast live stream with click-to-focus jumps.

### Winning

**Crown Index = Net Worth ÷ 10 + Market Share % × 3 + Civic Prestige × 4.**

The leaderboard and market-share bar update live in rank order, with an expandable breakdown
showing the three whole-number components summing exactly to the Crown Index. At the end of
Era IV, **The Mercadia Gazette — Final Edition** presents the winning House crest, narrative
headline, full standings breakdown, era-by-era market share chart, three match honours (*Corner
King*, *Price Shark*, *Civic Patron*), and Rematch or Continue in Endless actions.

## Modes

- **Solo.** One human against one to three AI Houses. **Standard** (4 deliberate eras),
  **Blitz** (4 brisk eras), or **Endless** (play past Era IV). Speed controls: Pause, 0.5×, 1×,
  2×, 3×.
- **Multiplayer room.** Two to four seats in any mix of humans and AI, plus one-click *No AI
  (Pure PvP)*. Authoritative server simulation with seamless reload and closed-tab seat reclaim,
  host migration, a high-contrast spectator notice for latecomers, and host pace controls (1×,
  1.5×, 2×).
- **Leaving.** Two-click confirmation (`Leave?` for four seconds) prevents accidental exits.

### AI Houses

- **Vesper (Aggressive Discounter):** Claims busy corners, undercuts rival shops, fires Promo
  Blitzes, and pivots to civic prestige in later eras if trailing.
- **Aurelia (Boutique Perfectionist):** Targets canal and plaza frontage, builds Bistros and
  Boutiques as they unlock, upgrades promptly to Level 3, and adapts pricing when queues stall.
- **Orson (Transit Expansionist):** Builds Pocket Parks and Tram Stops to manufacture corridors,
  then anchors them with high-throughput shops.

AI Houses respect the same Era unlocks, Charter rules, and deliberate pacing cadence as humans:
they plan ahead for Era transitions instead of spamming openings on second one, and remain
balanced across seeded Standard matches.

## What MERCADIA is not

- Not a frantic RTS click-fest. Deliberate pacing, Era Councils, and readable streets come first.
- Not a spreadsheet or tutorial wall. Guidance is brief, contextual, and self-retiring.
- Not a dark neon dashboard. The district lives in travertine, terracotta, sage, and canal teal
  under daylight and warm twilight lanterns.

## Quality bar

- The sunlit district is alive within one second of load, before any click.
- 85% or more of the viewport stays uninterrupted city during active play at 1440×900.
- Clean, unclipped layouts at 1440×900, 1024×768, and 390×844 (tabbed mobile dock, full-island
  Fit view, tap-preview-then-build, bottom-sheet Inspector, compact expandable Chronicle).
- Solo and multiplayer execute the same deterministic simulation core.
- Zero console errors, zero page errors, and zero failed WebSocket/network requests in solo and
  two-browser multiplayer play.
